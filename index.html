Sweet Words From Our Customers

Good to know

Questions about ordering? Start here, or message us on WhatsApp.

How do I place an order?

Choose a cake, fill in the booking form and review your summary. Then send it to us on WhatsApp. You don’t need an account.

How early should I book a cake?

The earlier the better, especially for celebration and custom cakes. Add your date to the booking form and we will confirm whether we can make it.

Can I request a customised cake?

Yes. Choose “Custom cake” in the form, tell us your idea, and we will quote you. You can send a reference photo in the WhatsApp chat.

Do you offer delivery?

You can choose delivery or pickup in the booking form. We will confirm what is available for your location when we reply.

How do I confirm my order?

Your order is confirmed only after we reply to you on WhatsApp with the details and price. Sending the form alone does not confirm it.

What payment methods are accepted?

We will share payment details with you on WhatsApp when we confirm your order.
